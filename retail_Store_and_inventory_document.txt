Supply Chain & Inventory Performance Analysis

Project Overview : 

This project analyzes retail store inventory and supply chain performance using Microsoft Excel.

The analysis focuses on inventory levels, product demand, sales performance, demand forecasting, stock availability, pricing, discounts, promotions, seasonality, and competitor pricing.

Dataset :

The dataset contains retail inventory and demand-related information.

Date
Store ID
Product ID
Category
Region
Inventory level
Units Sold
Units Ordered
Demand Forecast
Price
Discount
Weather Condition
Holiday/Promortion
competitor Pricing
Seasonality


Bussiness Questions :

What are total units sold and sales revenue by product category?
Which stores hold the highest inventory levels?
Which regions generate the highest units sold?
Which products have the highest actual demand?
How do units sold, units ordered, and inventory levels change over time?
How do discounts and holiday/promotional periods affect units sold and revenue?
How are seasonality, weather conditions, promotions, discounts, and competitor pricing associated with changes in demand?
